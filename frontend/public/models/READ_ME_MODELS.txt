To use Face Verification, you must download the following Face-API.js models and place them in this folder:

1. tiny_face_detector_model-weights_manifest.json
2. tiny_face_detector_model-shard1
3. ssd_mobilenetv1_model-weights_manifest.json
4. ssd_mobilenetv1_model-shard1
5. ssd_mobilenetv1_model-shard2
6. face_landmark_68_model-weights_manifest.json
7. face_landmark_68_model-shard1
8. face_recognition_model-weights_manifest.json
9. face_recognition_model-shard1
10. face_recognition_model-shard2

You can find these models at:
https://github.com/justadudewhohacks/face-api.js/tree/master/weights
